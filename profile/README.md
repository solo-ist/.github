Frontier models are inverting the physics of software development — months condense to
weeks, weeks into hours — while open-weight models keep getting cheaper, more capable and
more accessible. The barrier to entry for people without engineering experience is lower
than it has ever been.

This is a challenge to the enshittification pushed by Big Tech and Big AI: an **agentic
codebase**, written with AI as the primary developer and optimized for the model rather
than the human — not to line the pockets of the AI labs, but to empower the people who
benefit from free, open, transparent and *malleable* software.

*How* we build needs to align with *why* we build. Everything here is local-first,
open-source and MIT licensed, built for humans and agents alike.

## Shipping

### [Prose](https://github.com/solo-ist/prose) · [prose site](https://solo.ist/prose/)

A free, open-source markdown editor for the AI era. A clean, minimal base with *optional*
AI that **doesn't strive to write for you** — writing modes keep you in control, suggestion
and comment review let you keep up with feedback from humans *or* AI, and activity history
plus in-document provenance track where changes came from. Local-first, so your files stay
plain `.md`. An MCP server lets Claude Desktop read and edit your documents.

`Tauri` · `TypeScript` · `MCP` · `macOS`

## Coming soon

### Termy

A terminal app with a durable session plane where the agents actually run, on top of a
meta-harness that orchestrates across Claude, Codex, and increasingly capable open-weight
models. For humans who want to put AI to work for them — not become a reverse centaur, a
meat proxy for their LLMs.

`Tauri` · `React` · `Rust` · `tmux` — *repo is private until release*

### Solo Prime Coding Harness

A local-first, open-weight-centered orchestration layer for fleets and swarms of coding
agents. A Temporal-backed plane takes your intent as a goal and runs it as a loop — plan,
build, review, land — surviving restarts, crashes and the agent changing its mind. The
software factory the rest of these tools get built with.

`Temporal` · `TypeScript` · `Docker` · `Open Weight` — *repo is private until release*

## Light Phone III toolbox

Folk tech for a phone that ships without an app store.

- [**solophone**](https://github.com/solo-ist/solophone) — an opinionated loadout for the Light Phone III: setup, own tools, field notes
- [**lp3-menu**](https://github.com/solo-ist/lp3-menu) — a second toolbox: a curated, renameable menu of full Android apps
- [**lp3-notifications**](https://github.com/solo-ist/lp3-notifications) — a top-level notification screen: see, dismiss and manage without leaving the phone
- [**lp3-routine**](https://github.com/solo-ist/lp3-routine) — a step-by-step routine timer. No network, no dependencies
